Uncharted ground known gaps

Recorded and not settled. None of them affects a claim made today, but several will decide how M2 is built.

The schema cannot yet say

  • a prerequisite that applies to one route only
  • evidence from another page (link target, sample page, download)
  • two official readings in conflict, other than in prose
  • the order of checks that change the page
  • hover, viewport width, styles off, zoom, orientation, JavaScript off
  • one check result serving several requirements

Readings still open (all on “none”)

  • 10.5.3: wording against legibility
  • 11.9.2, 11.2.5: effective name against every present source
  • 7.3.1: one control or two
  • 1.9.x: is a caption necessary (Q47)
  • 11.2.2, 11.2.3: does a blank attribute bring a field into scope (Q48)
  • 3.1.x: not applicable, or validated

Oddities in the official source

  • Methodologies of 12.4.2 and 12.4.3 are swapped against their wordings
  • 10.5.2 and 11.10.6 copy a methodology step from a neighbour
  • One glossary anchor resolves to nothing
  • Writers followed the wording each time

Prerequisites with no engine behind them

  • keyboard and media need a pass that does not exist yet
  • state is met only on a declared state other than initial
  • model never gives a status; assistive_tech is never met in v1
